\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

All results come from three co-located arrays at a single desert site,
sharing one weather station, so their forecast errors are correlated
and the nine array-horizon cells are not nine independent trials. One
validation year and one held-out test year are used, at horizons of
one to six hours. Forecasts
are deterministic; no probabilistic evaluation is performed. These
results establish that the reference forecast and the treatment of
night hours materially change reported accuracy at this site - not
that the same magnitudes hold at a different site or climate.
